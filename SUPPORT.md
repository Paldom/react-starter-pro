# Support

## Before asking

- Read the [README](README.md) — setup, scripts, and architecture are covered
  there.
- Check [docs/assistant-ui.md](docs/assistant-ui.md) for assistant-ui
  integration details.
- Search [existing issues](https://github.com/Paldom/react-starter-pro/issues)
  — your question may already be answered.

## Asking a question

Open a [GitHub issue](https://github.com/Paldom/react-starter-pro/issues/new/choose)
and pick the question template. Include what you tried, what you expected, and
what happened instead — plus Node and npm versions if it's environment-related.

## Not here

- **Security vulnerabilities**: never via issues — see [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
- **Bug reports and feature requests**: use their dedicated issue templates
  rather than a question.

This is a solo-maintained project, so responses are best-effort.
